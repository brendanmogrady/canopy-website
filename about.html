Our Origin

Started in 2020 to provide services government clients needed but could not always find.

When Damon Steffens and Brian Swords left a large Florida state agency in 2020, they had spent years watching consulting firms work on the same problems they had lived from the inside. The firms were capable. The engagements were expensive. But something was consistently missing: the institutional knowledge of what it actually takes to move a budget through a legislative cycle, earn trust from career agency staff, and ship something that survives a change in administration.

What followed was a firm built on a different premise. Not former consultants who had learned government. Former government executives who had decided to consult. The distinction shapes every engagement: the scope, the timeline, the communication style, and the definition of done.

How We Work

Operator perspective. Consulting discipline.

Most consulting firms learn government by working in it. Canopy's leadership learned consulting by leaving government. That reversal shapes how we scope work, how we communicate with agency leadership, and what we consider a successful outcome.

We know what a program director needs to bring to a cabinet meeting. We know what a CFO needs to show an appropriations committee. We know what a CIO needs to say to survive a legislative IT review. We do not need that context explained to us — and that saves time, reduces rework, and produces implementations that last beyond the contract term.

We have sat on the other side of the table.

We have been the client — signed the procurement, defended the budget, and absorbed the consequences of a bad implementation. That changes every conversation about scope and timeline.

We work in the hard middle, not the easy edges.

Grants disbursement, financial reconciliation, audit-ready reporting, federal compliance — the unglamorous work where real value is created and where most implementations quietly fail.

We are specific about what we do not know.

We do not try to be everything to everyone. We are former Florida state executives who consult to government, nonprofit, and private-sector organizations across Florida and the Southeast. Knowing exactly where we add value is a feature, not a limitation.

We procure like a client needs us to.

GSA Schedule, TIPS cooperative purchasing, and Salesforce Implementation Ready Partner status — because procurement friction should not be the reason a good engagement doesn't start.
