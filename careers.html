Life at Canopy

Work that matters. Life that fits.

We are a small, senior-led team where everyone does real work and carries real responsibility. Here is what that actually looks like day to day.

Senior-led, not staffed-up

Our principals are former state CFOs and CIOs. You work alongside people who have actually run the agencies you are helping.

Collaborative by default

Small teams mean you are never siloed. You will know every project, every client, and every teammate — and they will know you.

Hybrid & flexible work

We structure engagements around outcomes, not seat time. Flexibility is built into how we operate, not something you have to negotiate for.

Deliberate development

We invest in clear career tracks, Salesforce certifications, and hands-on project exposure from your first week — not after you prove yourself.

Meaningful client work

Our clients are state agencies, nonprofits, and public institutions doing work that affects real people. The problems you solve here have consequences that matter.

Open door, direct culture

Leadership is accessible. If you have an idea, a concern, or feedback about how we do things — there is no chain to climb through to be heard.

Benefits

Built for the whole person.

We offer benefits that reflect what we actually value — your development, your health, and your time.

Salesforce Certifications — Paid

We sponsor Salesforce certification exams and training for eligible roles. Career development is not a reward here — it is part of the job from the start.

Comprehensive Health Benefits

Medical, dental, and vision coverage for you and your dependents. We contribute meaningfully to premiums — this is not a checkbox benefit.

401(k) with Company Match

We match contributions to help you build toward the future. Because the work we do for clients is long-term, we think the same way about our team.

PTO & Paid Holidays

Generous paid time off plus federal holidays. We want you rested — sustainable performance over the long run matters more than grinding through vacation days.

Hybrid & Remote Flexibility

Most roles are hybrid with genuine flexibility. We focus on the work, not the location. Client travel is project-dependent and always communicated in advance.

Professional Development

Beyond certifications, we invest in conferences, training programs, and internal mentorship. Growing your career is part of what you are hired to do here.

Project Manager
Tallahassee, FL Entry · Intermediate · Advanced Project Management

About the Role

Canopy Project Managers lead client-facing engagements across our IT, Finance & Administration, and Grants Management practice areas. You will manage scope, schedule, and stakeholder relationships for government and nonprofit clients navigating complex system implementations and organizational change.

We hire across experience levels — from early-career professionals building their first engagement portfolio to senior managers ready to lead multiple concurrent projects.

What You'll Do

  • Own project plans, status reporting, and stakeholder communication for active client engagements
  • Coordinate cross-functional teams including client staff, technical leads, and Canopy consultants
  • Identify and escalate risks before they become issues
  • Develop and maintain project artifacts: charters, WBS, RACI matrices, risk logs
  • Support business development efforts by contributing to proposals and scope estimates

What We're Looking For

  • Experience managing projects in IT, finance, or grants management contexts (level-dependent)
  • Strong written and verbal communication — you can explain a complex problem simply
  • Familiarity with state or local government operations is a meaningful plus
  • PMP, CAPM, or equivalent certification preferred at mid/senior levels

Entry-Level Salesforce Data Specialist
Tallahassee, FL Entry Level Salesforce

About the Role

This entry-level role is designed for someone who wants to build a career in Salesforce administration and CRM systems. You will support data migration and cleansing work on active Salesforce implementation projects — ensuring that client data arrives in the new system accurate, complete, and ready to use.

Canopy invests in Salesforce certifications for this role. If you are methodical, detail-oriented, and want to grow into a Salesforce Administrator or Consultant, this is the place to start.

What You'll Do

  • Analyze source data from legacy systems and map fields to Salesforce objects
  • Cleanse, transform, and validate data prior to migration using Excel, Google Sheets, or dedicated ETL tools
  • Load data into Salesforce using Data Loader or equivalent tools
  • Document data dictionaries, mapping specs, and migration runbooks
  • Support post-migration quality checks and issue resolution

What We're Looking For

  • High attention to detail — data quality work is unforgiving
  • Comfort with spreadsheets and structured data (Excel/Sheets proficiency required)
  • Familiarity with Salesforce or CRM systems is a plus, not a requirement
  • Eagerness to pursue Salesforce Administrator certification (we will support this)
