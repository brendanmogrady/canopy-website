Featured Case Study

The first Agentforce application reviewer in Florida state government.

A Florida state agency was processing grant applications through a 40-step manual intake process. Reviewers spent the majority of their time on completeness checks, eligibility routing, and document verification, work that had no policy discretion attached to it.

We designed and deployed an Agentforce agent that handles application intake, runs structured eligibility logic, routes exceptions to human reviewers, and produces an auditable decision record. It went into production, not a pilot environment, not a controlled demo, in a live grants cycle.

Program Management

Florida Clerks of Court Operations Corporation (CCOC)

Florida Guardianship Data Transparency Project

The Florida Clerks of Court Operations Corporation needed to procure a vendor to design and implement a centralized database consolidating guardianship data from across Florida's court systems, and build a public-facing website to meet its statutory transparency obligations. Canopy guided CCOC through every phase: procurement planning, RFP development, vendor evaluation, contract award, and post-award implementation oversight.

Following the successful contract award, CCOC engaged Canopy to provide ongoing project management advisory and oversight: keeping the implementation on track, applying best practices, and ensuring the final solution met CCOC's statewide service objectives.
