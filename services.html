Grants Management

Canopy has managed state and federal grant programs totaling billions of dollars — from FEMA disaster recovery to HUD community development to workforce programs.

From application through closeout — every phase, every requirement.

Grants management is not a side competency at Canopy — it is how several of our principals spent their careers inside Florida state government. We guide clients through the complete grant lifecycle, combining deep regulatory knowledge with hands-on operational experience managing programs at scale.

  • Grant Development & Application Support Structuring proposals, eligibility analysis, narrative development, and submission coordination.
  • Program Design & Subrecipient Management Establishing program structures, subrecipient agreements, monitoring plans, and risk assessments.

  • Federal & State Reporting SEFA preparation, SF-425 reporting, performance reporting, and all required agency submissions.
  • Monitoring, Audit Readiness & Closeout Internal monitoring, finding remediation, OIG audit response, and complete closeout documentation packages.
  • Grants Management Systems Salesforce-based grants management platform design, implementation, and ongoing support built specifically for government and nonprofit program administrators.

Organizational Change Management

Change is inevitable. Employee resistance and lost productivity are not. Canopy builds the human infrastructure that makes organizational change actually stick.

The part of transformation most firms skip over.

Technology implementations fail not because the system was wrong, but because the organization was not ready. Canopy has led change management for major IT programs, grants system overhauls, and agency restructuring — from the inside, as the people responsible for the outcome. We know what it takes to bring a workforce through change without losing momentum.

  • Organizational Readiness Assessment Baseline analysis of change capacity, stakeholder alignment, and cultural readiness before transformation begins.
  • Stakeholder Engagement & Communications Structured communication plans, executive alignment sessions, and change champion networks across the organization.
  • Training Strategy & Delivery Role-based training programs, job aids, and knowledge transfer plans designed for government and nonprofit audiences.
  • Process Redesign & Adoption Mapping current-state workflows, identifying gaps, and embedding new processes with measurable adoption milestones.
  • Transition & Sustainment Planning Ensuring changes hold after go-live — with governance structures, performance metrics, and ongoing reinforcement built in.

Change management is embedded in every Canopy engagement — not sold as a separate add-on. When we implement a system or redesign a process, we own the adoption outcome too.
